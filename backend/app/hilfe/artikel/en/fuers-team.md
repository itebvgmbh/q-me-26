Your shop uses Dranzeit for numbers and appointments and has invited you. With your own login, you can see under “My day” who's coming to you, on your phone or on a computer. You don't need an app.

## Create an account

1. Open the invitation link you received from your shop, by email or as a message.
2. Your email address is already filled in if the shop knows it. You can also use a different one.
3. Choose a password with at least 6 characters.
4. Tap **“Create account”**. You'll go straight to “My day”.

## Already have a Dranzeit account?

For example because you've booked an appointment before or work at another shop: tap **“Sign in and accept”** below the form and sign in. The invitation is then accepted straight away. If you're already signed in, just tap **“Accept invitation”**.

If you work at several shops, accept each invitation with the same account. Under “My day”, you choose the shop at the top.

The link is valid for 14 days and only for you. If it says “This invitation has expired” or “This invitation link doesn't exist”, ask your shop for a new link. Each new link replaces the old one.

## Signing in

Later, you sign in under [Sign in](/login) with your email address and password and go straight to “My day”. Forgotten your password? Enter your email address there and tap **“Forgotten your password?”**. You'll then get a link by email.

## My day

- **Time range:** At the top, you choose the day and whether you see 1, 3, 5 or 7 days.
- **Customers:** Each number and each appointment is a block on the timeline. Tap it and set the status: **“Waiting”**, **“Being served”**, **“Done”** or **“Cancelled”**. With “Being served”, the customer is notified if they've turned on notifications.
- **Add appointment:** Tap **“+ Appointment”** or a free spot on the timeline.
- **Move:** Drag a block to another spot. If the new time falls in a break, outside your working hours or on another appointment, Dranzeit won't allow it.
- **Breaks:** Hatched areas are your weekly breaks.
- **Working hours:** Under **“My working hours”** you set when you work. Customers can only book with you during these times.

More on this: [My day](/hilfe/mein-tag).

## What you see and what you don't

You only see your own customers, and “My day” hides cancelled ones. The front desk, the team, the services, the opening hours and the plan are managed by the person who set up the shop on Dranzeit. They also add your breaks for you. Talk to them if something isn't right there. You'll still find your own bookings as a customer under **“My appointments”**.
