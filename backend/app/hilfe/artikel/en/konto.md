For your shop, you need an account with an email address and a password. Your customers don't need one to take a number.

## Create an account

1. Go to [Sign up](/register-options) and tap **“I have a shop”**.
2. Enter your email address and a password with at least 6 characters.
3. Tap **“Create shop account”**. The setup wizard then starts.
4. We'll send you an email. Tap **“Confirm email address”** in it, and your address is confirmed.

People who work for you don't sign up themselves; they get an invitation from you by link or email. If the person already has a Dranzeit account, they accept the invitation with it. More on this: [Invite your team](/hilfe/team-einladen).

> **Important:** Whether an account belongs to a shop or to a customer is decided when you sign up. You can't change this yourself later. If you accidentally signed up as a customer, write to us at [hilfe@dranzeit.de](mailto:hilfe@dranzeit.de).

## Confirm your email address

After signing up, you'll get an email from us with the subject “Please confirm your email address”. In it, tap **“Confirm email address”**. This also works on a different device, for example on your phone. You can see whether your address is confirmed under **“My profile”**, directly below your email address.

It's not mandatory: booking, taking a number and your shop all work without it. But with a confirmed address, we know that it belongs to you. If a shop has added you to its customer list with this address, its appointments are added to your account as soon as you book there yourself.

No email? Check your spam folder. Under **“My profile”**, you can have it sent again with **“Resend confirmation email”**. If tapping the link shows “This link doesn't work.”, it has expired or has already been used. Then check in your profile whether your address is already confirmed, and if not, send yourself a new email.

## Signing in

Tap **“Sign in”** at the top (on a phone, in the menu), enter your email and password and tap **“Sign in”**. You'll go straight to the front desk under **“Today”** (or to whatever you've set the device up for), or to the setup wizard if you haven't created a shop yet.

If it says “Email or password is incorrect.”, check the spelling or reset your password. After too many attempts, you'll have to wait a moment.

## Forgotten your password

1. Go to [Sign in](/login) and enter your email address at the top.
2. Tap **“Forgotten your password?”**.
3. You'll get an email with a link where you can set a new password.

No email? Check your spam folder and whether the address was spelled correctly.

## Change your password

1. Open [My profile](/profile): tap the circle with your initial at the top right (on a phone, in the menu).
2. Under **“Change password”**, enter your **current password** and a **new** one with at least 6 characters.
3. Tap **“Change password”**.

On your other devices, you'll then need to sign in again with the new password. If you can't remember your current password, tap **“Forgotten your current password?”** there. You'll then get the same email as with “Forgotten your password”.

## Edit your profile and sign out

Tap the circle with your initial at the top right (on a phone, the menu), then **“My profile”**. There you change your **Name** and **Mobile number** and tap **“Save”**. The email address can't be changed here. If it has changed, write to us.

To sign out, tap **“Sign out”** in the same menu.

## Delete your account

We delete shop and team accounts on request, because the subscription, invoices and the shop's data are tied to them. Write to [st@mes-beratung.de](mailto:st@mes-beratung.de), ideally from your account's email address. What happens to your shop's data is explained under [Privacy: what data Dranzeit stores](/hilfe/datenschutz).

Customers delete their account themselves: under **“My profile”** → **“Delete account”** → **“Yes, delete account”**. Upcoming appointments are cancelled, and the name disappears from past ones.
