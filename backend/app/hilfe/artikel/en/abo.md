You take out your subscription under **“Plan”**. Payment is handled by Stripe via SEPA Direct Debit or card, in advance for one month or one year. You can cancel at any time, effective at the end of the paid period. All of this only works with the account that was used to create the shop.

## Take out a subscription

1. Tap **“Shop”** at the top, then **“Plan”**. **“My plan”** opens.
2. Choose **“Monthly”** or **“Yearly · 2 months free”**.
3. On the plan you want, tap **“Continue to payment”**. By doing so, you accept the [Terms](/agb).
4. At Stripe, enter your billing address and payment method, and your VAT ID (Umsatzsteuer-ID) if you like.
5. You're then back on “My plan”. The new plan appears there after a few seconds.

![“My plan”: where you stand now and the plans you can subscribe to.](/img/hilfe/tarif.jpg)

If your trial is still running, billing only starts when it ends; in the last two days of the trial, it starts straight away. If you have several shops, choose the shop at the top first: each shop has its own subscription.

## Change plan, payment method, invoices

While a subscription is running, “My plan” shows the button **“Manage subscription, payment method, cancel”**. It opens the Stripe customer portal. There you can:

- change your plan or billing interval (**“Switch”** on the plans takes you there too); Stripe charges or credits the difference pro rata
- change your payment method
- change your billing address and VAT ID
- view your invoices

You also receive every invoice by email. Under “My plan” → **“Invoices”** you can download every invoice yourself: as a PDF and as an **e-invoice** (XRechnung), which your accounting software or your tax adviser can import directly.

## Cancel

1. Under “My plan”, tap **“Manage subscription, payment method, cancel”**.
2. Cancel the subscription in the customer portal.

It keeps running until the end of the paid month or year. “My plan” then shows “Cancelled, ends on …” with the date. After that, your shop carries on with the [Free plan](/hilfe/gratis-tarif), and nothing is deleted. If you don't cancel, the subscription renews for the same period each time.

## If something goes wrong

- **“The last payment is outstanding”:** Open the customer portal and check your payment method. If the payment remains outstanding despite a reminder, our Terms allow us to move the shop back to the Free plan.
- **“Payment isn't working right now”:** Try again a little later.
- **“Payments aren't set up yet”:** Write to us at [hilfe@dranzeit.de](mailto:hilfe@dranzeit.de).
