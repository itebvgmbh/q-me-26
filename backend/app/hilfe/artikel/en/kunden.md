**“Customers”** in the menu at the top lists all of your shop's customers. Tap a name to see how often they've been in, when they were last in, what they usually have done, and every visit with its price. Your notes are there too. Employees also find “Customers” in the menu. The tablet at the front desk doesn't show the customer file.

## Who's on the list

- Customers that you or your team add under **“Add customer”**.
- Anyone who books or takes a number with you using an account.
- Anyone who books or takes a number without an account and gives their name and mobile number. If that number is already in your list, the visit goes to that customer.
- Anyone who books via the [phone assistant](/hilfe/telefon-assistent).

You assign walk-in customers who only give a name yourself at the front desk (see below).

## Search and groups

At the top, you search by name, phone number or email. How the number is written doesn't matter: “0170 123” also finds “+49 170 123…”.

The buttons below show groups:

- **Regulars:** at least 3 visits.
- **New:** at most one visit, for the first time in the last 60 days.
- **Not seen in a while:** away for longer than usual and no appointment planned. If someone comes about every 4 weeks, they appear here after 6 weeks. With only one visit, after 90 days.

With **“Sort”**, you order by “Last visit”, name, revenue or visits.

## What's on the customer page

- **Visits:** past appointments that weren't cancelled.
- **Last visit** and **Usually comes:** how long ago it was and how often the customer comes on average, from 2 visits.
- **Revenue:** the prices of their visits according to your price list. Dranzeit doesn't see what was actually paid in the shop, for example with a discount or tip.
- Below that, what they usually have done and with whom, and how often they've cancelled or not shown up.
- **Upcoming appointments** and the **History** with service, person and price, newest first. Appointments made via the phone assistant have a phone icon.

With **“Edit”**, you change the name, phone number and email address. If the customer has linked their account, the email address belongs to the account and stays as it is.

## Notes

- **Note for the team:** what everyone should know, for example “likes it short on the sides”. Write it in the field and tap **“Save note”**.
- **Note for visit:** In the history, tap **“Note for visit”** on a visit, for example for the colour formula: “7/1 + 6 %, 30 min”. Next time, it's right there with that visit.

Only your team sees notes, never the customer and never the phone assistant. Don't enter any health information, such as allergies: you'd need the customer's explicit consent for that.

## Assign a customer at the front desk

If the customer under **“Now serving”** isn't assigned to anyone in your list yet, for example a walk-in, tap **“Assign customer”** below. Dranzeit suggests customers with the same mobile number or the same name. You can also search, or create a new entry with **“Add as new customer”**. The visit then appears in their customer file. Nothing changes on the appointment itself.

If the customer is already assigned, their name appears there. Tapping it opens their customer page, for example for the note for the visit.

## Delete a customer

At the very bottom of the customer page, tap **“Delete customer”** and confirm with **“Yes, delete”**. Their notes are then gone, upcoming appointments are cancelled and past ones lose the name. If the customer has linked their account, only the entry disappears from your list; their appointments stay in their account.

Dranzeit automatically deletes customers without a linked account if there hasn't been an appointment with them for 12 months. More: [Privacy](/hilfe/datenschutz)

## On the Free plan

The list and the appointments per customer are available on every plan. Visits, revenue, groups and notes are part of the Solo plan and the larger ones. More: [Plans](/hilfe/tarife)
