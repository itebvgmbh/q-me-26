You change all your shop's details in one place. Changes apply immediately, on your shop page too.

## How it works

1. Tap [Shop](/shop-profile) at the top, then **“Edit”**.
2. Change whatever you need.
3. At the very bottom, tap **“Save changes”**.

## The fields

- **Shop name** and **Email** must be filled in.
- **Address:** street and house number, postcode, town. Without an address, you won't appear on the map, see [Help customers find you](/hilfe/gefunden-werden).
- **Owner's name, Phone, Website:** Of these, customers only see the phone number on your shop page.
- **Opening hours:** see [Set your opening hours](/hilfe/oeffnungszeiten).
- **Category:** The suggestions for services and the filter on the map are based on this. The “Walk-in hairdressers” city pages only list shops with the category “Hairdressers/Barbers”. The category appears on your shop page below the name.
- **Logo:** Tap **“Upload logo”** and choose an image file of up to 1 MB. Dranzeit shrinks JPG and PNG files to a maximum of 200 pixels in width and height; a square image works best. Use the X at the top right of the image to remove it again.
- **Lead time for online appointments:** This is how many minutes in advance online bookings must be made. With 30, at 10:00 the earliest time available online is 10:30. Taking a number and appointments you add yourself always work straight away. 0 means: no lead time.
- **Description:** one or two sentences about what makes your shop special.

> **Important:** A new logo is only saved once you then tap “Save changes”.

## Where customers see this

Name, address, phone, category, logo and description appear on your shop page. You can see what it looks like under “Shop” → **“Customer view”**. The logo also appears on the [waiting room display screen](/hilfe/bildschirm).

The QR code stays the same, whatever you change here. Only if you change the name should you reprint the [QR poster](/hilfe/plakat): the name is at the top of it.
