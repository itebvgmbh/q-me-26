If someone without a phone is standing in front of you, you add them to the queue yourself. Dranzeit finds the next free slot, just like with the QR code. That way everyone is in the same queue, whether they scanned or not.

## How it works

1. Under [Today](/shop-dashboard), tap **“Add customer”** at the top. In the window, **“Number (next slot)”** is selected.
2. Choose the **service**. Dranzeit immediately shows when it'll be the customer's turn and with whom.
3. Under **“With whom?”**, **“Anyone – whoever is free first”** is preselected. If the customer wants a specific person, choose them. Only people who do this service are listed.
4. In **“Name to call out (optional)”**, enter what you'll call them later, for example “Mr Krause”.
5. Tap **“Issue number”**.

![“Add customer” with “Number (next slot)”: service, person and, optionally, a name. The next free slot is shown straight away.](/img/hilfe/nummer-vergeben.jpg)

To confirm, Dranzeit shows the **number** in large print, along with when and with whom it's the customer's turn, for example “No. 42 · today at 10:30 with Anna”. Tell the customer the number and time, ideally on a slip of paper. Tap **“Done”** to close the window, or **“Another number”** to issue the next one straight away.

In the person's column, the customer now appears under **“Up next”**, sorted by their time, for example as “Mr Krause · No. 42”. On the [waiting-room display](/hilfe/bildschirm), only the number is shown. That's why it matters for customers without a phone: that's where they see when it's their turn.

If you're running behind today, the new number gets a time after the people already waiting, the way it will really turn out. More on this: [How Dranzeit calculates the waiting time](/hilfe/wartezeit).

> **Tip:** If you have several customers without a phone, enter a name. The number is always shown as well, but “Mr Krause” is easier to call out than “No. 42”.

A customer you issue a number to doesn't get any message on their phone. They wait in the shop or come back at the time you told them. They're called like everyone else: [Call customers and mark them as done](/hilfe/aufrufen).

## When nothing is free today

If there's no slot left today, Dranzeit shows the next one on another day and adds: “Nothing more is free today.” You can still issue the number; it then applies to that day.

If instead you see “Nothing free in the next 14 days” or “No one in the team offers this service”, something is missing in the setup: check under **“Shop”** → **“Team”** whether the person does this service and has working hours. More on this: [Working hours and breaks](/hilfe/arbeitszeiten-und-pausen).
