Everyone who serves customers needs an entry in the team, including you. There are only free slots for people with working hours and services. Only people who should see their own day under “My day” need their own account.

## Add a person

1. Under **“Shop”**, open the [Team](/staff-management) and tap **“Add person”**.
2. Enter the **Name**. Email, phone and role are optional.
3. Under **Services**, everything is ticked. Untick whatever the person doesn't do.
4. The **Working hours** are prefilled with your opening hours. Adjust the days and times.
5. Tap **“Add”**. You can then invite the person straight away (see below) or tap **“Done”**.

If you serve customers yourself, add yourself with your own email address. You'll then see your customers straight away under **“Today”** with **“Only me”** and under “Shop” → “My day”, without an invitation. If you have several shops and work in more than one of them, add yourself like this in each one.

## Invite people so they can see their day

If you already entered the person with an email in the setup wizard, their invitation went out by email when you opened the shop. The final page shows who it worked for.

Otherwise, there are two ways. Both link exactly this team entry to the person's account.

- **Invite by email:** In the team, tap **“Invite by email”**, enter name and email and tap **“Send invitation”**. The person appears in the team straight away, with all services and with your opening hours as working hours. Adjust both afterwards under **“Edit”**.
- **Send the link yourself:** Straight after “Add person”, or later under **“Edit”** in the **“Profile”** tab under “Invitation”: tap **“Create invitation link”** and then **“Copy link”** (on a phone, also **“Share”**). Send it via WhatsApp, text message or email. No email needs to be entered in the team for this.

You can see straight away in the window whether the email went out. If it didn't, it says why, along with the link for you to pass on yourself.

The person creates their account with the link. If they already have one, for example because they've booked with you before or work at another shop, they sign in with it and accept the invitation. What they see afterwards is explained in [For your team](/hilfe/fuers-team).

In the team list, the person shows **“Invited”** while the invitation is open and **“Has access”** once it has been accepted.

A link is valid for 14 days and belongs to exactly this person. If you create a new one or send the email again, the old link no longer works. Expired? Simply create a new one under “Edit”.

> **Tip:** If someone works in several shops that use Dranzeit, each shop invites them, and they accept all invitations with the same account. Under “My day”, they then choose the shop at the top.

## How many people?

The Free and Solo plans include one person, the Shop plan up to four, the Team plan up to ten, and the trial ten as well. You count too if you've added yourself. Once the limit is reached, a notice with **“Change plan”** appears instead of the buttons. More on this: [Plans](/hilfe/tarife).

## Deactivate and restore a person

If someone no longer works for you, tap **“Deactivate”** next to the person and confirm. After that, nobody can book with them; appointments already booked remain. If they have an account, they no longer see “My day”.

Deactivated people are listed at the bottom of the team list under **“Deactivated”**. With **“Restore”**, everything is back as it was, including their access. They then count towards the people on your plan again.

For holidays or sick leave, it's better to use breaks: [Working hours and breaks](/hilfe/arbeitszeiten-und-pausen).
