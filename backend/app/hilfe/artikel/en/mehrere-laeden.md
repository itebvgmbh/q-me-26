With one account, you can run several shops. Each shop has its own opening hours, services, team, QR code and plan. You can see them all under **“My shops”**.

## All shops at a glance

Tap your account at the top right, then [My shops](/laeden). Each shop is listed there with its address and plan. **“Open”** takes you to that shop's front desk, **“Manage”** to its settings under **“Shop”**.

## Set up another shop

1. Under [My shops](/laeden), tap **“Set up another shop”**.
2. The setup wizard starts just like the first time: find your shop, details, category, opening hours, services and team. How that works is explained in [Ready to go in 10 minutes](/hilfe/in-10-minuten-startklar).
3. At the end, tap **“Open shop”** and then **“Go to front desk”**.

## Switch between shops

If you have several shops, the name of the selected shop appears in the header at the top, next to the logo. Tap it and choose another shop – or **“All shops …”** for the overview. Everything below then shows that shop: **“Today”**, **“Calendar”** and **“Shop”** with team, services, statistics, plan, poster and phone assistant.

The device remembers the shop you selected last. If a tablet stays in one shop, select that shop at the top once on the tablet.

## Delete a shop

1. Under [My shops](/laeden), tap **“Delete”** next to the shop – or, under **“Shop”**, tap **“Delete shop …”** at the very bottom.
2. You see what goes with it: appointments and tickets, team, services, customer list, logo, photos, QR code and links.
3. To confirm, type the shop's name and tap **“Delete permanently”**.

Your account stays, and so do your team's accounts. Customers with an upcoming appointment are not notified – cancel with them yourself first.

> **Important:** If the shop still has a running subscription, cancel it under [Plan](/tarif) first. A cancelled subscription runs until the end of the paid period; after that, nothing more is charged. If the phone assistant is booked, cancel it first as well.

## What applies to each shop separately

- **Plan:** Every new shop can try everything for 30 days from setup. After that, it carries on with the Free plan or gets its own subscription. You take that out under [Plan](/tarif) after selecting the shop at the top. More on this: [Plans](/hilfe/tarife).
- **Team:** Each person belongs to exactly one shop. Nobody can be added to a second shop with the same email address, except you.
- **QR code and link:** Each shop has its own. Put up each shop's own [QR poster](/hilfe/plakat) in that shop.
