With one account, you can run several shops. Each shop has its own opening hours, services, team, QR code and plan.

## Set up another shop

1. Tap [Shop](/shop-profile) at the top, then **“Another shop”**.
2. The setup wizard starts just like the first time: find your shop, details, category, opening hours, services and team. How that works is explained in [Ready to go in 10 minutes](/hilfe/in-10-minuten-startklar).
3. At the end, tap **“Open shop”** and then **“Go to front desk”**.

## Switch between shops

If you have several shops, the name of the selected shop appears in the header at the top, next to the logo. Tap it and choose another shop. Everything below then shows that shop: **“Today”**, **“Calendar”** and **“Shop”** with team, services, statistics, plan, poster and phone assistant. **“Edit”**, the QR code and **“Open waiting-room display”** also apply to this shop.

The device remembers the shop you selected last. So the front desk starts with the shop you last had open. If a tablet stays in one shop, select that shop at the top once on the tablet.

## What applies to each shop separately

- **Plan:** Every new shop can try everything for 30 days from setup. After that, it carries on with the Free plan or gets its own subscription. You take that out under [Plan](/tarif) after selecting the shop at the top. More on this: [Plans](/hilfe/tarife).
- **Team:** Each person belongs to exactly one shop. Nobody can be added to a second shop with the same email address, except you.
- **QR code and link:** Each shop has its own. Put up each shop's own [QR poster](/hilfe/plakat) in that shop.

If you work in several shops yourself, [“My day”](/hilfe/mein-tag) shows your day in the shop selected at the top.
