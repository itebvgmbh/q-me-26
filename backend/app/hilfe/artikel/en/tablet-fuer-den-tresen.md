A simple tablet with 10 to 11 inches is enough for the front desk. Dranzeit runs in the browser, so you don't install anything. We recommend the **Samsung Galaxy Tab A11+** for around €200: it gets seven years of updates and can be set to charge only to 80%. That's easier on the battery, because the tablet is plugged in all day.

## Which device

- **Our recommendation:** [Samsung Galaxy Tab A11+](https://geizhals.de/3647384), Wi-Fi, 6 GB/128 GB, around €200.
- **If you like Apple:** [iPad (A16)](https://geizhals.de/3433234), from around €430. The iPad can also be set to charge only to 80%.
- **Better not:** Amazon Fire tablets (ads on the lock screen, no Chrome), no-name tablets (no updates) and older second-hand iPads (no charge limit).

Already have a tablet? Try it out: if Dranzeit runs there in an up-to-date Chrome or Safari, it's good enough.

## What else you need

- a stand for 11 inches, for example [this aluminium one](https://www.amazon.de/dp/B09VZ1CBH1)
- an angled 3 m USB-C cable, for example [this one](https://www.amazon.de/dp/B085G36K9V), because the socket is rarely right at the front desk
- a 25 W power adapter, if there isn't one in the box, for example [the one from Samsung](https://www.amazon.de/dp/B0CBDWQVSF)

With the Galaxy Tab, everything together costs around €240. Prices as of November 2026. The links are examples; we don't earn anything from them.

## Setting it up

1. Plug the tablet into the charging cable and connect it to your shop's Wi-Fi.
2. Limit charging to 80%: on the Galaxy Tab, **“Settings”** → **“Battery”** → **“Battery protection”** → **“Maximum”**; on the iPad, **“Settings”** → **“Battery”** → **“Battery Health”** → **“80% Charge Limit”**.
3. Open dranzeit.de in Chrome or Safari and add Dranzeit to your home screen: in Chrome via **⋮** → **“Install app”** or **“Add to Home screen”**, in Safari via **“Share”** → **“Add to Home Screen”**. Then open Dranzeit via the new icon.
4. Tap **“Sign in”** and, below the form, **“Sign in with your phone, no password”**. The tablet shows a QR code and a check number.
5. Scan the QR code with the camera of the phone on which you're signed in to Dranzeit. If the phone shows the same check number, tap **“Sign in tablet”**. If you have several shops, choose the right one first.

The tablet now shows the front desk and always starts there. It has never seen your password. It can only do **“Today”** and **“Calendar”**: appointments, numbers and customers. Plan, team, services and settings stay on your phone. As long as the front desk is open, Dranzeit asks the tablet not to turn off the screen.

> **Tip:** To stop anyone switching to other apps by accident, you can pin Dranzeit in place. On the Galaxy Tab, search the settings for **“Pin app”**; on the iPad, it's called **“Guided Access”** under **“Accessibility”**.

## Signing out a tablet

Under **“Shop”** → **“Front desk tablets”**, you see all paired tablets. With **“Sign out”**, you sign one out, for example if it's broken or lost. It then shows the QR code again.

> **Important:** If you change your password, all devices are signed out, including the tablets. Just pair them again afterwards.
