Most customers understand Dranzeit in one sentence when someone says it to them. In the first few weeks, explain it to everyone who pays at your till. After that, the poster on the door explains it by itself.

## What to say at the till

To repeat word for word:

*“Next time you don't have to sit here: scan the code on the door and take a number. Then you'll see on your phone roughly when it's your turn, and you can go and grab a coffee in the meantime. You don't need an app for it.”*

If your plan includes notifications (from Solo upwards), add:

*“We'll let you know shortly before it's your turn. You can switch that on right on the page.”*

## Customers without a smartphone

Nobody has to scan. Anyone who comes without a phone gets their slot from you: under “Today”, tap **“Add customer”**, enter their name and tell them the time Dranzeit shows you. They're then in the same queue as everyone else. More on this: [Issue a number at the front desk](/hilfe/nummer-vergeben).

## Regular customers

- Anyone who prefers a fixed time chooses **“Preferred time”** when taking a number and books an appointment, even without an account. For this, your plan must include online appointments (from Solo upwards). Or you add them yourself: [Add an appointment](/hilfe/termin-eintragen).
- With an account, regulars see everything under “My appointments” and can use **“Earlier slot”**. More on this: [Earlier slot](/hilfe/frueher-dran).
- There's a separate guide for customers, and you can pass its link on: [How your number works](/hilfe/so-funktioniert-die-nummer).

## Text for Instagram, WhatsApp and Google

You'll find your link for taking a number under [Shop](/shop-profile) in the box **“Take a number via QR code”**. Tap **“Copy link”**. The link goes straight to your shop, just like the QR code.

Suggested text to paste:

*Don't fancy waiting? Take a number with us online, no app and no sign-up needed. You'll see straight away roughly when it's your turn, and just come by then. Here's how: (paste your link here)*

Where to put it:

- **Instagram:** in your bio and as a link sticker in a story.
- **WhatsApp:** as a status, just paste the text and link.
- **Google Business Profile:** as a post with a link.

> **Tip:** Take a test number yourself with your phone via the QR code beforehand. Then you'll know what your customers see. How to get rid of it afterwards is explained under [Test run before your first customer](/hilfe/testlauf).
