Dranzeit makes you a ready-made A4 poster with your QR code. Customers scan it with their phone camera and go straight to taking a number at your shop, without an app and without an account.

## Printing

1. Tap [Shop](/shop-profile) at the top and, next to the QR code, **“Print poster”**.
2. If you have several shops, choose the right one at the top.
3. Tap **“Print”**. Your browser's print window opens; the poster is already set to A4 portrait.
4. Choose your printer and print.

![The poster: shop name, QR code and three steps for your customers.](/img/hilfe/plakat.jpg)

> **Tip:** If you want the yellow frame around the QR code to be printed, switch on “Background graphics” in the print window (in Chrome and Edge under “More settings”). There you can also switch off “Headers and footers”.

## Where to put it up

- **On the door or in the shop window, facing out:** anyone passing by who sees that it's busy takes a number straight away and comes back later.
- **At the front desk:** for everyone who's already inside.
- **In the waiting area:** a second poster, if customers sit there.

## So the code scans well

- Hang it at eye level, about 1.20 to 1.60 metres.
- Make sure there's light and avoid reflections. Behind glass or laminated, it reflects easily; matt film helps.
- Don't print the poster smaller than A4.
- After putting it up, check it once with your phone from one or two metres away.

The QR code stays the same, even if you change your address, opening hours or services. You only need to reprint it if your shop's name changes: it's at the top of the poster.

## Share the link too

Under **“Shop”**, in the **“Take a number via QR code”** box, you'll find **“Copy link”**. The link leads to the same place as the QR code. Put it in your Google Business Profile, your Instagram bio or your WhatsApp status. More on this: [Help customers find you](/hilfe/gefunden-werden). For your own website, there's a ready-made box: [Dranzeit on your website](/hilfe/auf-der-webseite).
