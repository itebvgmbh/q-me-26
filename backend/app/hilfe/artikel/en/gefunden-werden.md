Customers find you on Dranzeit in three ways: via the map [Find shops](/shop-map), via the city pages “Walk-in hairdressers in …” and via your own link. Dranzeit needs your address for the map and the city pages.

## 1. Enter your full address

1. Tap [Shop](/shop-profile) at the top, then **“Edit”**.
2. Fill in **Street and house number**, **Postcode** and **Town**.
3. Tap **“Save changes”**.

With an address, your shop appears as a dot on the map. Customers search there by town or postcode, or tap **“Near me”**. If the address is missing, you'll see a notice under “Shop”.

## 2. Choose your category

In the same window, you choose your **Category**. Customers can filter by it on the map.

So far, city pages only exist for hairdressers and barbershops. If “Hairdressers/Barbers” is set as your category (or none yet) and your town is filled in, your shop appears on your city's page, e.g. dranzeit.de/friseur-ohne-termin/koeln. It shows how many people are waiting with you right now, when you're open today, and a button for taking a number. The pages are made for search engines. Whether and when Google shows them is up to Google. [See all cities](/friseur-ohne-termin)

## 3. Keep your shop page up to date

Every shop has its own page with logo, address, phone, description, services with duration and price, opening hours and team. You can see what it looks like under “Shop” → **“Customer view”**. A logo and two sentences of description make it more personal: [Change name, address, logo and description](/hilfe/laden-bearbeiten).

## 4. Share your link

Under **“Shop”**, in the box **“Take a number via QR code”**, tap **“Copy link”**. The link takes people straight to taking a number at your shop. Good places for it:

- **Google Business Profile:** as a link for appointments or as your website
- **Instagram:** in your bio
- **WhatsApp:** in your status or as a reply when someone asks whether it's busy right now
- **Your website:** as a “Take a number” button

In the shop itself, the [QR poster](/hilfe/plakat) helps.
