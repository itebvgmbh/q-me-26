Dranzeit sends your customers messages when it matters to them: shortly before it's their turn, when you call them, before a booked appointment, when you cancel or move something and when an earlier slot opens up. Each one is sent only once. This is part of the plans from Solo upwards and is included in the trial. In the Free plan, customers don't get any messages.

## The messages

- **“You're up soon”** is sent when the customer is next in line and their turn is expected in 45 minutes at most. If someone is still ahead of them, it comes about 10 minutes beforehand. If you're running behind, it follows the recalculated time, see [How Dranzeit calculates the waiting time](/hilfe/wartezeit). Text: “You're next. Time to head over.” or “Only 2 ahead of you. Time to head over.”
- **“It's your turn”** is sent as soon as you call the customer at the front desk: “… is calling you. Come in now.” If you set the customer back to “Waiting” and call them again later, it's sent again.
- **“Reminder of your appointment”** is sent two hours before a booked appointment, if it was booked at least three hours beforehand: “…, today at 14:00. Can't make it? Then please cancel.”
- **“Cancelled”** is sent when you cancel a number or an appointment, including with **“No-show”** at the front desk: “… has cancelled your number today at 14:00.”
- **“New time”** is sent when you move an appointment to another time, in “My day” or via “Edit”: “… has moved your appointment: now today at 15:30 with Anna (previously 14:00).”
- **“Earlier slot?”** is sent when an earlier slot opens up and the customer wants one: “…: today at 10:30 there's a free slot (instead of 11:15). Reserved for you until 10:12.” More on this: [Earlier slot](/hilfe/frueher-dran).

Your shop's name always comes first. When the customer taps the message, their number page opens; for the earlier-slot offer, “My appointments” opens.

Dranzeit doesn't send anything for appointments on past days, even if you cancel them afterwards.

## Push or email: the customer chooses

After taking a number or booking, the customer sees the **“Get notified”** box on their number page:

- **“Turn on notifications”** sends the message straight to their phone, like an app. The browser asks for permission once.
- With **“By e-mail”**, they enter their address, just for this number. Dranzeit deletes it no later than 90 days after the appointment.

If they switch on neither, they don't get anything. But the number page updates itself, so they can also just keep an eye on it. Customers with an account get the reminder, cancellation, new time and earlier-slot offers at their account's email address without having to do anything.

## iPhone

On the iPhone, Apple only allows browser notifications for apps on the Home Screen. That's why Dranzeit shows iPhone customers the email field straight away. Advise them: enter an email address, done.

## Who doesn't get a message

- Customers to whom you issue a number under “Today” with “Add customer”, or for whom you add an appointment without an account. They don't have their own number page. Tell them the number and the time, see [Issue a number at the front desk](/hilfe/nummer-vergeben).
- Customers without an account who have switched on neither push nor email.
