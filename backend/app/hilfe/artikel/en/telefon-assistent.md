If nobody in the shop picks up, the phone assistant answers the call. Your customers keep dialling your usual number. All you do is set up call forwarding with your phone provider.

## What it does on the phone

- Offer free appointments and book them once the caller agrees
- Tell callers the current waiting time and take a number for the queue
- Answer questions about opening hours, address, services and prices

It speaks German and says in its first sentence that it's an AI. Appointments and numbers then appear in the calendar and at the front desk like all the others.

## Recognising regulars

If someone books by phone, the assistant adds them to your customer list, with the name from the conversation and the number they're calling from. If the number is already there, for example because you added the customer yourself, it uses that entry.

If they call again, the assistant greets them by name, doesn't ask for it again and offers them the same service as last time. It also knows their upcoming appointments and can tell them about them.

You can see all of a person's appointments under **“Shop” → “My day” → “Customer list”**: tap the name. You can also delete an entry there. Without a linked account, Dranzeit deletes it automatically if there hasn't been an appointment for 12 months. If someone calls with a withheld number, the assistant doesn't create an entry.

## Call list and calling back

Under **“Shop” → “Phone assistant”**, you can see under **“Calls”** who called and what came of it: the booked appointment, the number taken or “No appointment booked”. If it says “Announcement only”, the caller only heard the short announcement, for example because the trial minutes were used up.

Where no appointment came of it, you can call the person directly with **“Call back”**. The list shows calls from the last 90 days; Dranzeit deletes older ones. If you delete a customer from the customer list, their calls disappear too.

If a very large number of shops are on the phone with the assistant at the same time, the caller briefly hears “One moment, please” and is then connected. If that doesn't work either, they hear an announcement that all lines are busy, and the call appears in your list.

## What it costs

- **Trial:** 30 minutes free. The trial ends by itself, and no subscription is created.
- **Add-on:** €29 net a month with 100 minutes, then €0.20 per minute. It's counted to the second, and the month's total is rounded up to full minutes. The add-on is available from the Solo plan upwards and is not included in the founder price.
- **Cost cap:** Unless you set your own, it costs at most €59 a month, which is 250 minutes. You can set the cap yourself between €29 and €500. Once it's reached, callers only hear a short announcement until the end of the month. You never pay more than the cap.
- **Invoice:** The add-on runs as a separate monthly subscription alongside your plan, with its own invoice. It lists the base price and the minutes as separate items, with the 100 included minutes already deducted.

At 80% and at 100% of the trial minutes or the cap, you get an email and a notice at the front desk and under “Shop”.

## Try it and book it

1. Open **“Shop” → “Phone assistant”** and tap **“Try it free”**. At the moment, we're still activating the assistant shop by shop. To get it, write to us at [hilfe@dranzeit.de](mailto:hilfe@dranzeit.de).
2. Set it up as described below and try it out with real calls.
3. If you want it to keep answering, tap **“Book add-on”**. You need a subscription from Solo upwards for this. Payment is handled by Stripe, using the same payment method as your plan.

## How to set it up

1. Under **“Shop” → “Phone assistant”**, enter the number your customers call and tap **“Switch on”**. The assistant only answers calls that are forwarded from this number.
2. Set up call forwarding to the number shown. To do this, choose how you make calls in the shop: **“Mobile”**, **“Telekom landline”**, **“Fritzbox”** or **“Other provider”**. For mobile and Telekom landline, you also choose **“Only when no one answers”** or **“Immediately”**. With “Immediately”, your phone no longer rings, and every call goes straight to the assistant. Switch off any answering machine or voicemail, otherwise it will answer the call before it reaches the assistant.
3. Call your shop from another phone and don't answer. As soon as the assistant picks up, the page shows **“Active”**.

> **Tip:** If nothing comes through on the test call, some providers don't pass on your shop's number. Write to us, and you'll get your own number to forward to.

## Let your customers know

As soon as the page shows **“Active”**, you'll find ready-made texts there for your Google listing, your website and the counter. On the [QR poster](/hilfe/plakat), you can add a line with your number. Your customers will also automatically see **“Book by phone (in German)”** on your shop page, in the shop list and when booking. For this, your shop details must include the number you're forwarding.

On Google, only ever enter your shop's number, never the assistant's number.

## Change the cost cap and cancel

You change the cap under **“Shop” → “Phone assistant”**. A higher cap applies immediately, even if the old one had already been reached.

You can cancel there with **“Cancel at the end of the month”**. It keeps answering until the end of the paid month; after that, callers only hear a short announcement pointing them to online booking. If your plan subscription ends, the add-on also ends at the end of its month.

## Switch off

Under **“Shop” → “Phone assistant”**, tap **“Switch off”**. Also remove the call forwarding with your provider. As long as it's in place, callers only hear a short announcement pointing them to online booking. They hear the same if the trial minutes are used up, the cap is reached or a payment is outstanding. After switching off, also remove the notices for your customers, for example on Google and on the poster. Switching off doesn't cancel the add-on.
