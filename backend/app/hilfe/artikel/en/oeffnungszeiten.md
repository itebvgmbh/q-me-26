Your opening hours determine when people can take a number or book an appointment with you at all. Customers see them on your shop page.

## How to change them

1. Tap [Shop](/shop-profile) at the top and then **“Edit”**.
2. Under **Opening hours**, use the switch to set each day to **open** or **closed**, and enter **from** and **to**.
3. If you close at lunchtime, tap **“Lunch break”** for that day. The default is 12:30 to 13:30; adjust the times. One break per day is possible.
4. The same every day? Set up the first open day of the week and tap **“Use Monday's hours for all open days”**. If you're closed on Mondays, it shows your first open day instead, e.g. “Use Tuesday's hours …”. The lunch break is copied too.
5. Tap **“Save changes”**.

At least one day must be open, and the break must be within the opening hours. If something doesn't fit, Dranzeit tells you when you save.

## Good to know

- **The lunch break applies to everyone in the team.** Breaks for individual people are covered in [Working hours and breaks](/hilfe/arbeitszeiten-und-pausen).
- **Your team's working hours don't change with it.** If, for example, you're now also open on Saturdays, switch on Saturday for the people in your team too. Otherwise nobody can book.
- **Customers who have already booked stay.** If a number or an appointment is now outside the opening hours, Dranzeit doesn't cancel it on its own. You do that in the **“Calendar”**.

## Public holidays, shop holidays, special opening hours

Dranzeit can't save exceptions for individual dates yet. Here's how to do it anyway:

- **Closed for one day:** Enter a one-off break covering the whole day for every person in the team: “Shop” → “Team” → “Edit” → **“One-off breaks”** tab.
- **Open longer once, or on a day you're normally closed:** Change the opening hours and the working hours of the people who'll be there. Afterwards, set both back, because the times apply every week.
