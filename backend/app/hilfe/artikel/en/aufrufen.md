At the front desk, each person on your team has their own column. One button in it calls the next customer and marks the one before as done. That's all you need day to day.

## A person's column

- At the top you'll see the name, how many are still waiting and how many are done. If the person is running behind, you'll see something like **“approx. 15 min delay”** underneath.
- **“Now serving”** shows who is being served right now, with the service and since when.
- **“Up next”** shows the next six people waiting, in order of time. The first one is marked **“Next”**. Each one shows whether they have a **number** or an **appointment** and, if there's a delay, the new time too, such as “approx. 10:45”.

![A person's column: who is being served at the top, below that the people waiting in order of time.](/img/hilfe/tresen-schlange.jpg)

Customers with a number are shown by their number, with their first name if they gave one: “Lena · No. 42” or just “No. 42”. This also applies to numbers you issue at the front desk. The customer appears under this number on the [waiting room display screen](/hilfe/bildschirm) too.

If the customer gave a mobile number when taking a number or in their profile, it's shown underneath. Tap it to call them, for example if they're not there after you've called them up. Dranzeit deletes the phone number after 90 days.

Anyone waiting stays in the column until you call them or cancel them, even if their planned time has already passed.

If you serve customers yourself, tap **“Only me”** at the top: you'll then see only your own column. **“All”** shows the whole team again.

## Calling

1. In the column, tap **“Call next number”**. The top customer in “Up next” moves to “Now serving”.
2. When you're done with them and someone else is waiting, the button reads **“Done – next number”**. One tap marks this customer as done and calls the next one.
3. If nobody else is waiting, it just reads **“Done”**. Tap it for the last customer too, otherwise they'll be missing from the customers done and from **Revenue** under “Calendar”.

![After calling: Lea is being served, the button now reads “Done – next number”.](/img/hilfe/tresen-aufgerufen.jpg)

Calling only works under **“Today”**. You'll find other days under **“Calendar”**, where this button isn't available.

## If the next customer isn't due until later

Without asking, the button only calls customers who are scheduled within the next 15 minutes. If the next one is an appointment in the afternoon, the button just reads **“Done”**, and underneath it shows who's coming next and when. If the customer is already there, tap **“Call now anyway”** and confirm. Dranzeit asks the same when nobody is being served and you tap **“Call next number”**.

## If someone doesn't turn up

If you've called someone and they're not there, give them a quick call if a mobile number is shown. If they don't come, tap **“No-show”** under their name in “Now serving” and confirm. Dranzeit cancels them, their slot is freed up, and they're notified if they've turned on notifications. Then call the next customer as usual.

Cancel customers who aren't coming any more. Otherwise they stay near the front of the queue, and everyone after them sees a later time.

## What your customer sees

Their number page immediately shows **“It's your turn. Come on in.”** If they've turned on notifications, the message “It's your turn” arrives on their phone or by email, a second time too if you set them back to “Waiting” and call them again later. After “Done”, the page shows “All done. Thanks for your visit!” More on this: [Which messages your customers receive](/hilfe/benachrichtigungen).

## Changing a status by hand

Under **“Calendar”**, in the **“Day”** view, the list **“All appointments on …”** at the bottom shows all of the day's customers. You expand it with **“All appointments as a list – for editing”**.

1. Tap **“Status”** next to the customer.
2. Choose **Waiting**, **Being served**, **Done** or **Cancelled**.

If you set someone to **Cancelled**, they disappear from the column and their slot is freed up. The customer is notified if they've turned on notifications or have an account. Use **“Edit”** to change the time, person or service. The customer is told about a new time too.
