On the Free plan, every number and every appointment in the current calendar month counts, except cancelled ones. On the 1st of the next month, the count starts again from zero.

## What counts

- Numbers that customers take via QR code
- Numbers you issue under **“Today”** with **“Add customer”**
- Appointments you add with **“Add appointment”**
- everything with the status **Waiting**, **Being served** or **Done**

So customers who didn't turn up count too, as long as the number isn't set to **Cancelled**. An appointment counts in the month in which it takes place, not in the month in which it was added.

> **Tip:** Test numbers you take yourself count too. Cancel them and they drop out again. More on this: [Test run before your first customer](/hilfe/testlauf).

## Where you can see the count

At the top of the front desk, you'll see something like “Free plan: 42 of 100 customers this month.” You'll find the same figure under **“Plan”** at **“Customers this month”**.

## When you reach 100

- The front desk shows: “You've reached this month's 100 free customers. Nobody can take a number online right now.”
- Anyone who scans the QR code sees this instead of the button: “… has no more numbers available online right now. Please ask in the shop.”
- Under **“Today”**, you can keep adding customers to the queue with **“Add customer”**.

On the 1st of the next month, customers can take numbers online again automatically. If you don't want to wait that long, choose a subscription: from Solo upwards, there's no limit. More on this: [Take out, change and cancel a subscription](/hilfe/abo).

## What else the Free plan doesn't include

Online appointments, notifications, Earlier slot, the waiting room display screen and statistics are only available on the paid plans, and there's room for one person on the team. You'll find an overview under [Which plan suits me?](/hilfe/tarife).
