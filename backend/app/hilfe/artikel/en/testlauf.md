Run through the process yourself once before real customers come. You need the front desk on a computer or tablet, plus your phone. It takes five minutes.

> **Tip:** Do the test while your shop is open according to your opening hours and you're in according to your working hours. Otherwise, the number lands on the next free day and you can't call it today.

## 1. Take a number

1. Open [Today](/shop-dashboard), the front desk. The **“Ready to go”** list at the top shows you the QR code.
2. If you're signed in to Dranzeit on your phone, sign out there first. Otherwise, Dranzeit books the number to your account and adds you as a customer.
3. Scan the QR code with your phone camera. Choose a service and then **“Anyone – as long as it's quick”** or a person.
4. Enter a first name such as “Test” and tap **“Take a number”**. Your phone now shows **“You're in the queue.”** with your number.
5. It appears immediately at the front desk under **“Up next”**.

![On the phone: enter a first name, then “Take a number”.](/img/hilfe/kunde-bestaetigen.jpg)

If you've hidden the list, you'll find the QR code under **“Shop”** in the box **“Take a number via QR code”**. With **“See the customer view”** below it, you can see the customer page on your computer. But don't take a number there while you're signed in.

## 2. Try out notifications

On your phone, under **“Get notified”**, tap **“Turn on notifications”** and allow them. On an iPhone, this doesn't work in the browser; use the email field there instead. Because you're first in the queue, “You're up soon” usually arrives in less than a minute.

On the Free plan, this box is missing: notifications are available from Solo upwards and during the trial.

## 3. Call the number

1. At the front desk, tap **“Call next number”**. Your phone shows **“It's your turn. Come on in.”**, and as a message too if notifications are on.
2. Tap **“Done”**. The phone shows “All done. Thanks for your visit!”.

![After calling, the phone shows “It's your turn”.](/img/hilfe/kunde-dran.jpg)

## 4. Remove the test number again

A completed test number counts like a real customer: under “Today” in “done”, in the calendar in “Done” and “Revenue”, in the statistics and, on the Free plan, among the 100 customers a month. Numbers can't be deleted, but they can be cancelled. Cancelled ones don't count.

1. Open [Calendar](/kalender) and tap **“All appointments as a list – for editing”** at the bottom.
2. Next to the test number, tap **“Status”** and then **“Cancelled”**.

In the statistics, it then only appears under “Cancelled”.

> **Tip:** If you only want to test taking a number, tap **“Leave the queue”** and **“Yes, leave”** on your phone. This works as long as the number is still waiting, and cancels it straight away.
