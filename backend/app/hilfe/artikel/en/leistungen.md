Services are what customers choose when they take a number or book. The most important thing is the **duration**: Dranzeit uses it to work out when someone's turn is.

## Add a new service

1. Under **“Shop”**, open [Services](/service-management) and tap **“Add service”**.
2. Enter the name, duration and price. The other fields are optional.
3. Tap **“Add service”**.

![A service with duration, preparation and clean-up, and price.](/img/hilfe/leistung.jpg)

The fields:

- **Duration (minutes):** how long you work on the customer.
- **Preparation and clean-up (minutes):** time for sweeping, disinfecting or tidying up. When customers take a number or book online, Dranzeit adds it to the duration. So a haircut with 30 minutes and 5 minutes of preparation and clean-up takes up 35 minutes. Customers only see the 30.
- **Price (€):** 0 is allowed too, e.g. for a first consultation.
- **Description:** shown next to the service on your shop page. Good for notes like “Price from €40, depending on hair length”.
- **Category:** for sorting, e.g. Women or Men. It only appears in your list; customers don't see it.
- **Shown in search under:** the term under which customers find the service in [Find shops](/shop-map), e.g. “Men's haircut” or “Beard & shave”. More on this in the next section.

## Which term your service is found under

Every shop names its services differently. So that search still works, Dranzeit assigns each service to a fixed term, such as “Men's haircut”, “Colour & highlights” or “Manicure”. The name and category are usually enough for this. That way, anyone searching for “Men's haircut” also finds your “Clipper cut”. The list shows for each service which term it's found under.

If the assignment doesn't fit, tap **“Edit”** and choose the right term under **“Shown in search under”**. With **“Don't assign to a group”**, the service can only be found by its own name. With **“Automatic”**, Dranzeit assigns it itself again.

## Change a service

Tap **“Edit”** next to the service, change what's needed and tap **“Save changes”**. This applies to new bookings. Anyone who already has a number keeps their time.

## Delete a service

1. Tap **“Delete”** next to the service.
2. Read the confirmation question: it says how many upcoming appointments already have this service.
3. Tap **“Delete”**.

Customers can then no longer book the service, and it disappears from the people in your team. Appointments already booked with this service remain. Deleted it by mistake? At the bottom, under **“Deleted services”**, tap **“Restore”**; the assignment to people is back as well.

## Who does which service?

You set this for each person: [Team](/staff-management) → **“Edit”** → **“Services”** tab, switch on or off, then **“Save changes”**. For each service, customers only see the people who offer it.

A newly added service isn't assigned to anyone yet. As long as that's the case, Dranzeit offers it with everyone. So assign it straight away if not everyone does it.

## Suggestions and price list in the setup assistant

When you set up a shop, Dranzeit suggests typical services for your trade, with the usual duration and price. You tick what fits and change the duration and price directly.

If you have a website with a price list, an AI can read services, durations and prices from it. This takes up to a minute. It recognises prices like “from €40” and puts them in the description. Who runs the AI is stated in the [privacy policy](/datenschutz).

> **Important:** The AI can get things wrong. The duration in particular is rarely on websites and is then estimated; it's marked “– estimated”. Check every line before you continue.
