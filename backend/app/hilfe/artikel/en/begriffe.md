The app and the help always use the same words. Here's what they mean.

### Subscription

Your shop's paid plan: Solo, Shop or Team, monthly or yearly. You take it out under “Plan” and cancel it at the end of the paid period. [Take out, change and cancel a subscription](/hilfe/abo)

### Statistics

Shows how many customers come, how long they wait and when it's busiest. You'll find it under “Shop” → “Statistics”, from the Shop plan upwards. [Which plan suits me?](/hilfe/tarife)

### Invitation link

A link for a person in your team. With it, they create their own account and then see their day under “My day”. [Invite your team](/hilfe/team-einladen)

### Earlier slot

Customers with an account can switch on, when booking or later under “My appointments”, that they're offered an earlier slot if one opens up. Their place stays safe until they accept. From the Solo plan upwards. [Earlier slot](/hilfe/frueher-dran)

### Almost your turn

The “You're up soon” message that customers get by push or email shortly before it's their turn. When you call them, “It's your turn” follows. Both from the Solo plan upwards, and only if the customer has switched it on. [Notifications](/hilfe/benachrichtigungen)

### Free plan

The free plan for one person and up to 100 customers a month, without online appointments and notifications. After the trial, every shop without a subscription carries on in it. [Free plan](/hilfe/gratis-tarif)

### Founder offer

For the first 100 shops: 3 months free, then the Shop plan for €19 instead of €39 a month (excl. VAT). [Founder offer](/hilfe/gruenderangebot)

### Today

That's what the front desk is called in the menu: just today, one column per person. If you're in the team yourself, “Only me” shows only your own customers. [Call customers](/hilfe/aufrufen)

### Calendar

In the menu for owners: here you browse through the days, as “Day” with one column per person or as “Week” for one person. This is where you add appointments and edit them in the list “All appointments on …”. [Add an appointment](/hilfe/termin-eintragen)

### Customer

Anyone who has a number or an appointment with you, with or without an account. [How the number works](/hilfe/so-funktioniert-die-nummer)

### Shop

Your business on Dranzeit, with address, opening hours, services and team. With one account, you can run several shops. In the menu under “Shop”, you'll find everything for managing it: team, services, plan, QR code and more. [Edit your shop](/hilfe/laden-bearbeiten)

### Service

What you offer, for example a haircut, with duration and price. Dranzeit uses the duration to work out when someone's turn is. [Add services](/hilfe/leistungen)

### My day

A person's timeline with all the day's appointments and numbers. Each person in the team sees their own customers there. Staff find it at the top of the menu, owners under “Shop” → “My day”. [My day](/hilfe/mein-tag)

### Number

A place in the queue without a fixed time. Customers take one via QR code, or you issue it under “Today” with “Add customer”. Dranzeit also shows roughly what time it'll be their turn. [How the number works](/hilfe/so-funktioniert-die-nummer)

### Opening hours and working hours

Opening hours apply to the whole shop, working hours to each person individually. Customers only get a slot when both fit. If a person has no working hours, nothing is free with them. [Opening hours](/hilfe/oeffnungszeiten), [Working hours and breaks](/hilfe/arbeitszeiten-und-pausen)

### Person and team

A person is anyone who serves customers at your shop, including you. All the people together are your team. How many there can be depends on the plan. [Invite your team](/hilfe/team-einladen)

### QR poster

An A4 poster with your shop's QR code. Customers scan it and take a number. You print it under “Shop” → “Print poster”. [Print and put up the poster](/hilfe/plakat)

### Queue

Everyone waiting for one person today, in the order in which they'll be served. Each person has their own queue. [Call customers](/hilfe/aufrufen)

### Status

At the front desk, every number and every appointment has a status: **Waiting**, **Being served**, **Done** or **Cancelled**. Customers with an account see **Booked**, **It's your turn**, **Done** and **Cancelled** instead under “My appointments”. [Call customers](/hilfe/aufrufen)

### Appointment

A slot with a fixed time. Customers book it online from the Solo plan upwards, even without an account, or you add it in the “Calendar” with “Add appointment”. [Add an appointment](/hilfe/termin-eintragen)

### Trial

The first 30 days after you open your shop. You can use everything as in the Team plan, without payment details. [Trial](/hilfe/testphase)

### Front desk

Your view in the shop, called “Today” in the menu: for each person, you see who's being served and who's next, and you call the next customer with “Call next number”. [Call customers](/hilfe/aufrufen)

### Preparation and clean-up

Minutes that Dranzeit adds to the duration of a service, for example for tidying up. You set them for each service. [Add services](/hilfe/leistungen)

### Lead time

How many minutes in advance a customer has to book online. It doesn't affect taking a number or appointments you add yourself. You set it under “Shop” → “Edit”. [Edit your shop](/hilfe/laden-bearbeiten)

### Waiting-room display

A view for the TV in your shop: who's being served right now, who's next, and a QR code for taking a number. You open it under “Shop” → “Open waiting-room display”, from the Shop plan upwards. [Waiting-room display](/hilfe/bildschirm)
