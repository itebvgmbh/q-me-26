If you work alone, Free or Solo is enough. If there are two to four of you, take Shop; for up to ten people, Team. Everyone who serves customers at your shop counts as a person, including you. There's no commission: you pay a fixed price, no matter how many customers come via Dranzeit.

## Free – €0

Free of charge with no expiry date, for one person and up to 100 customers a month. Customers take a number via QR code, you call them at the front desk, and your shop appears on the map. Online appointments, notifications, Earlier slot, the waiting room display screen and statistics are not included. What counts towards the 100 customers is explained under [Free plan](/hilfe/gratis-tarif).

## Solo – €19 a month

For one person, unlimited customers. Plus:

- Online appointments and the queue in one day
- Earlier slot
- “Almost your turn” message by push or email
- Appointment reminders

## Shop – €39 a month

For up to 4 people, everything in Solo plus:

- Team: each person sees their own customers under “My day”
- Waiting room display screen
- Statistics: waiting time, customers per day

This is the typical barbershop.

## Team – €59 a month

For up to 10 people, otherwise exactly like Shop. More than 10 people? Write to us at [hilfe@dranzeit.de](mailto:hilfe@dranzeit.de).

## Paying yearly and VAT

If you pay yearly, you pay 10 monthly prices, so you get 2 months free: Solo €190, Shop €390, Team €590 a year.

All prices are net, plus VAT. If you're a small business exempt from charging VAT (Kleinunternehmer) and can't reclaim input VAT, the gross price is what counts for you:

- Solo: €22.61 a month or €226.10 a year
- Shop: €46.41 a month or €464.10 a year
- Team: €70.21 a month or €702.10 a year

If you run several shops, each one has its own plan.

> **Tip:** You don't have to decide straight away. For the first 30 days, you can use everything as on the Team plan, without payment details. More on this: [Trial](/hilfe/testphase).
