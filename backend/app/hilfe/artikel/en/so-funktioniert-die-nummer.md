You scan the QR code in the shop, choose what you'd like done and take a number. Your phone shows you roughly when it'll be your turn. Until then, you can go wherever you like.

## Take a number

1. Scan the QR code on the door or at the front desk with your phone's camera.
2. Under **“What would you like done?”**, choose your service.
3. Under **“With whom?”**, choose a person or **“Anyone – as long as it's quick”**. Each person shows their next free slot.
4. Under **“When suits you?”**, you see roughly what time it'll be your turn. Enter your first name and tap **“Take a number”**. Your mobile number is optional.

![After scanning, you choose what you'd like done.](/img/hilfe/kunde-leistung.jpg)

Rather have a fixed time? If the shop offers appointments, tap **“Book an appointment”** at the top below the heading, or in the last step choose **“Preferred time”** instead of **“As soon as possible”**. Pick a day and time and tap **“Book an appointment”**. You don't need an account for this either.

No QR code to hand? On dranzeit.de, tap **“Take a number”** or **“Book an appointment”** in the menu and choose your shop.

## While you wait

Your number page shows your number, how many people are ahead of you and **“Your turn approx.”** with the time, plus a time window such as “Expect your turn between 11:52 and 12:10.” It updates itself. When the shop calls you, it says **“It's your turn. Come on in.”** Closed the page? You'll find it again on the Dranzeit home page on the same day, on the same phone. Or keep the page's link (as a bookmark or on your Home Screen): with it, you can also give up your number.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an app or an account?

No. Everything runs in your phone's browser, and you don't need an account for a number. With an account, you see your numbers and appointments under “My appointments” and can use “Earlier slot”. You can also book fixed appointments without an account if the shop offers them. Already have a number? Tap **“Create an account and keep the number”**.

### How is the “approx.” time worked out?

Every service has a planned duration. Dranzeit plans everyone ahead of you with the same person and gives you the next free slot after them. If things take longer ahead of you, Dranzeit recalculates: your number page then shows a later time and how much later it'll be. It will never show a time earlier than the one you got when you took your number. Still, it's best to be back a few minutes early.

### How do I find out when it's my turn?

On your number page, in the **“Get notified”** box, tap **“Turn on notifications”** and allow the messages. You'll then get “You're up soon” shortly before it's time, and “It's your turn” when the shop calls you. If the shop cancels your number or moves your appointment, you'll be told as well. Instead of push, email works too: enter your address and tap **“Save”**. If you don't see the box, your shop doesn't offer messages. In that case, check the page now and then.

### Does it work on an iPhone too?

Yes, it's best to use email there. Apple only allows browser notifications for apps on the Home Screen. That's why Dranzeit shows you the email field straight away on an iPhone.

### What if I'm late?

Your number doesn't expire on its own. If the shop calls you and you're not there, they can cancel it and call the next people. If you've turned on notifications, you'll be told. Let the front desk know as soon as you're there. The shop then decides when it's your turn.

### Can I give up my number?

Yes. On your number page, tap **“Leave the queue”** and confirm with **“Yes, leave”**. Your place is then freed up. Without an account, this works on the phone you took the number with, or via the link to your number page – a link from a notification email only shows when it's your turn. Otherwise, let the front desk know. With an account, you can also do it under “My appointments” with **“Cancel appointment”**.

### Can I take a different time?

Yes, if the shop offers online appointments. On your number page or under “My appointments”, tap **“Change time”** and pick another time, with a different person if you like. Your current place stays until the new one is confirmed. After that, you have a fixed appointment, and “Earlier slot” is then off. Even when you tap **“Leave the queue”**, Dranzeit offers you another time with **“Rather another time?”**. Without an account, this works just like giving up your number: only on the phone you took the number or booked with, or via the link to your number page.

### What happens to my data?

Without an account, Dranzeit stores the service, person, time and your first name, plus your mobile number, email address or push registration if you provide them. Only the shop sees your name and mobile number, so they can call you if you're not there when you're called. Other customers and the waiting-room display only see your number. Names and contact details are removed 90 days after the appointment, the push registration after 3 days.

With an account, your name stays in your appointments; the mobile number and email address on the appointment are also removed after 90 days. You delete your account under **“My profile”** → **“Delete account”**. There are no advertising cookies. More in the [privacy policy](/datenschutz).

### What is “Earlier slot”?

If an earlier slot opens up, Dranzeit offers it to you. For this, you need an account and tick **“Earlier slot if one opens up”** when taking a number or booking, if the shop offers it. Forgot, or did the shop add the appointment for you? Under “My appointments”, every upcoming appointment has the **“Earlier slot if one opens up”** switch; use it to turn this on or off. You'll then get the “Earlier slot?” message by email to your account, or as a push if you've turned on notifications. The offer appears under “My appointments”, which also shows until when it's reserved for you. With **“Take the earlier slot”**, it's yours; until then, your current place stays safe.
