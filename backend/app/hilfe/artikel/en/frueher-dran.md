If a customer cancels, a gap opens up. “Earlier slot” fills it automatically: Dranzeit offers the freed slot to customers who are scheduled later and would like to come earlier. You don't need to do anything.

## Who can take part

- Your plan includes “Earlier slot”: from Solo upwards and during the trial.
- The customer has an **account** and has switched on “Earlier slot” for their appointment.

It doesn't work without an account, because the offer is accepted under “My appointments”.

## How it's switched on

- **When taking a number or booking an appointment:** The customer ticks **“Earlier slot if one opens up”**.
- **Later under “My appointments”:** Every upcoming appointment has the switch **“Earlier slot if one opens up”**. With it, the customer switches it on or off again themselves, up to 15 minutes before the start. This also works for appointments you've added for them, as soon as they've linked their account via your link ([My day](/hilfe/mein-tag), section “Add customer”).
- **By you in the calendar:** in the list **“All appointments on …”** under **“Edit”** → **“Notify if an earlier slot opens up”**. This only works for customers with an account.

If a customer switches it off while a slot is being offered to them, the offer disappears and the slot goes to the next person. If a customer picks a different time themselves with **“Change time”**, “Earlier slot” is switched off afterwards.

## How it works

1. A slot becomes free, for example because someone cancels, leaves the queue, you move an appointment or a customer is done early.
2. Dranzeit checks straight away, or after 5 minutes at the latest, who is waiting for an earlier slot with the same person. A slot is only offered if the whole service fits into it and it's at least 15 minutes in the future.
3. Whoever of them is scheduled earliest gets the slot first, reserved just for them for 15 minutes.
4. The customer immediately gets the message **“Earlier slot?”** with the new time: as a push notification if they've turned on notifications for their number, otherwise by email to their account. If they tap it, “My appointments” opens with **“Earlier slot?”**, where they tap **“Take the earlier slot”**. Until then, their current slot stays safe.
5. If they accept, their old slot becomes free and goes to the next person in the same way. If they don't accept, it's the next person's turn after 15 minutes.

At the front desk you only see the result: the customer appears in their column at the new time.

## Done early counts too

If a customer is done earlier than planned and you tap **“Done”**, the rest of their time is free again. The same applies if you call a customer with a fixed appointment before their time: their planned time becomes free. If the freed slot is at least 15 minutes in the future, “Earlier slot” offers it.
