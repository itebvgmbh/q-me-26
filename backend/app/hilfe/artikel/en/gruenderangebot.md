The first 100 shops to accept the founder offer use Dranzeit free for 3 months with all features. After that, they get the Shop plan for €19 instead of €39 a month, or €190 instead of €390 a year, as long as their subscription runs without interruption. All prices excl. VAT.

## What you do in return

- Dranzeit's QR poster hangs visibly in your shop, for example on the door.
- In the first 3 months, you tell us once a month what bothers you.
- We may name your shop as a reference. You can withdraw this at any time.

## How to claim your spot

1. Create your shop if you haven't already: [Ready to go in 10 minutes](/hilfe/in-10-minuten-startklar).
2. Tap **“Shop”** at the top and then **“Plan”**.
3. In the **“Founder offer: … spots left”** box, tap **“Claim a founder spot”**.

After that, **Founder** appears next to your plan under “My plan”. The 3 months count from that day; a trial that's still running is included in them.

You can claim the spot as long as there are spots left and your shop doesn't have an active subscription. How many spots are still free is shown on the [pricing page](/preise).

## After the 3 months

The subscription doesn't start on its own. Take out the **Shop** subscription under **“Plan”**; the founder price is already shown there. You can also do this during the 3 months: billing only starts afterwards, except in the last two days, when it starts immediately. Without a subscription, your shop carries on in the [Free plan](/hilfe/gratis-tarif) after the 3 months.

The founder price only applies to the Shop plan. Solo and Team cost the normal price for founders too.

> **Important:** In Stripe's customer portal, only the normal prices are shown when you switch. If you're a founder and want to change your plan or billing interval later, write to us first at [hilfe@dranzeit.de](mailto:hilfe@dranzeit.de).
