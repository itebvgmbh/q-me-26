Customers can only take a number or book with a person when the shop is open, the person is working and they're not on a break. Dranzeit always uses the time when all three fit.

## Opening hours and working hours

- **Opening hours** apply to the whole shop. You set them under **“Shop” → “Edit”**: [Set opening hours](/hilfe/oeffnungszeiten).
- **Working hours** apply to one person: **“Shop”** → [Team](/staff-management) → **“Edit”** → **“Working hours”** tab. For each day there's a switch and a from–to time, then **“Save changes”**. Each person can also change their own hours under “My day” → **“My working hours”**.

Example: The shop is open from 9:00 to 18:00, Lena works from 12:00 to 18:00. With Lena, there are slots from 12:00.

> **Important:** If you open longer or on an extra day, the working hours don't change with it. Adjust them in the team, otherwise the new time stays empty.

## Three kinds of breaks

- **The shop's lunch break:** in the opening hours, one per day. It applies to everyone.
- **Weekly breaks** for a person, e.g. every Wednesday from 13:00 to 14:00: Team → “Edit” → **“Weekly breaks”** tab. Choose the weekday, **“Add break”**, enter the start and end time, **“Save”**. With the switch in front of it, you pause a break without deleting it.
- **One-off breaks** on a specific date, e.g. a doctor's appointment: Team → “Edit” → **“One-off breaks”** tab. Choose the date, **“Add new break”**, enter the times, **“Save”**.

![A person's breaks: under “Team” → “Edit”, tab “One-off breaks” or “Weekly breaks”.](/img/hilfe/team-pausen.jpg)

Breaks are saved straight away. You don't need “Save changes” for them.

## Holidays and sick days

There's no separate holiday feature yet. Here's how to do it:

- **Single days:** For each day off, enter a one-off break covering the whole working time, e.g. from 9:00 to 18:00.
- **Away for longer:** Switch the days off in the working hours. Switch them back on afterwards, because working hours apply every week.

Don't use “Deactivate” for this: the person would then no longer see “My day” either, and you'd have to restore them under “Deactivated” afterwards.

## What about people who have already booked?

Breaks and new times only apply to new bookings. Anyone who already has a number or an appointment stays booked in. Dranzeit doesn't cancel anyone on its own. If an appointment falls within a weekly break, it's marked as **“Conflict”** under “My day”.

Move these customers under **“Calendar”** in the list **“All appointments on …”** via **“Edit”**, or cancel them there via **“Status”** → **“Cancelled”**. You open the list at the bottom with **“All appointments as a list – for editing”**. The customer sees the cancellation on their number page. If they have notifications on, they also get a message.
