If a customer wants a fixed time, for example on the phone, you add the appointment yourself. Dranzeit only lets you choose times when the person is really free. That way, you can't double-book anything. On the day of the appointment, the customer is in the same queue as the numbers.

## How it works

1. Under [Calendar](/kalender), tap **“Add appointment”** at the top. In the window, **“Fixed time”** is selected. It works the same way under **“Today”**: **“Add customer”** and then **“Fixed time”**.
2. Choose the **Service**.
3. Under **“With whom?”**, choose the person. For an appointment, you pick a specific person; there's no “Anyone” here.
4. Enter the **Name**. If you've added the customer before, choose them under **“Existing customer (optional)”**, and the name is filled in straight away.
5. Choose the **Date** and then the **Time**. Only this person's free times can be selected, in 15-minute steps.
6. **Duration** and **Price** come from the service. Only change them if it's different this time.
7. Tap **“Add appointment”**.

To confirm, you'll see something like “Appointment added: 24/10 at 14:00.” On the day of the appointment, the customer appears in the person's column under “Up next”, marked **Appointment**.

> **Tip:** If the preferred time isn't there, someone is already booked then or the person is on a break. If there are no times at all that day, the person isn't working then or the shop is closed. Try another person or another day.

## What the customer gets to know about it

Nothing. Dranzeit doesn't send them a confirmation or a reminder, because they don't have their own page for an appointment you've added. Tell them the day and time on the phone.

If they'd like a reminder, they can book the appointment online themselves, even without an account. For this, your plan must include online appointments (from Solo upwards). If they turn on notifications on their number page or have an account, they get a reminder two hours beforehand. More on this: [Which messages your customers receive](/hilfe/benachrichtigungen).

## Change or cancel an appointment

In the **“Calendar”**, go to the day of the appointment and tap **“All appointments as a list – for editing”** at the bottom. In the list **“All appointments on …”**, tap **“Edit”** next to the customer to change the time, person or service, or **“Status”** → **“Cancelled”**.

Your team can also add appointments themselves, directly in their own timeline: [My day](/hilfe/mein-tag).
