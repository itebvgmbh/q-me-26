From the day you open your shop, you can use everything Dranzeit can do for 30 days, just like in the Team plan. You don't need payment details for this. The trial ends on its own and costs nothing.

## What you can do during the trial

- up to 10 people in the team, each with their own “My day”
- unlimited customers
- online appointments, Earlier slot, “Almost your turn” messages and appointment reminders
- waiting-room display and statistics

How many days you have left is shown under **“Plan”**. In the last week, a notice at the top of the front desk also reminds you.

## What happens afterwards

If you don't choose a subscription, your shop automatically carries on in the [Free plan](/hilfe/gratis-tarif). Then:

- at most 100 customers a month
- customers can no longer book appointments online, only take numbers
- no more “Almost your turn” messages and reminders, not even for appointments that are already booked
- Earlier slot, waiting-room display and statistics are off
- there's room for one person in the team

## What stays

Nothing is deleted. Shop, services, opening hours and all appointments stay as they are. If you have more people in the team than the plan allows, they stay in. You just can't add any new ones. If you choose a subscription later, everything is unlocked again, including the statistics with your previous figures.

## Taking out a subscription during the trial

You can do this at any time under **“Plan”**. You only pay once the trial is over, so you don't lose any trial days. Exception: in the last two days of the trial, billing starts immediately. How the subscription works: [Take out, change and cancel a subscription](/hilfe/abo).

> **Tip:** With the [founder offer](/hilfe/gruenderangebot) for the first 100 shops, you test for 3 months instead of 30 days.

Shops that were already on Dranzeit before 20 October 2026 get a 30-day trial from that day.
