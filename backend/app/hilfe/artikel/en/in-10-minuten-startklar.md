All you need is a phone or a computer with internet access, your opening hours and a rough idea of what you offer and how long it takes. Nobody has to install an app, neither you nor your customers.

## 1. Create an account

Go to [Sign up](/register-options), choose **“I have a shop”** and enter your email address and a password. You don't need payment details: you can try everything free for the first 30 days.

## 2. Set up your shop

The setup wizard starts straight afterwards. It asks you one question per screen:

1. **Find your shop:** Enter your shop's name. If it's listed on Google, tap it – address, phone, website and opening hours are copied over. Not on Google? Tap **“Enter it yourself”**.
2. **Check the details:** Name and email are enough. But add your address too, otherwise customers won't find you on the map.
3. **Choose a category:** This lets us suggest suitable services.
4. **Opening hours:** Choose a template or set each day individually, with a lunch break if you like.
5. **Services:** Tick suggestions or add your own. The **duration** is what matters – Dranzeit uses it to work out when it's someone's turn. If you have a website with a price list, Dranzeit can read it.
6. **Who works with you?** Say whether you serve customers yourself and who else does. For each person, you set which services they do and on which days they're in. Anyone you give an email address to automatically receives their invitation when you open the shop.
7. **Open shop:** Check the summary and tap **“Open shop”**.

> **Tip:** You don't have to get everything perfect. You can change any detail later under “Shop”, where you'll also find “Services” and “Team”.

More on this: [Opening hours](/hilfe/oeffnungszeiten), [Add services](/hilfe/leistungen), [Invite your team](/hilfe/team-einladen).

## 3. Try it out yourself once

Before your first customer arrives, run through it once. It takes two minutes and clears up any uncertainty:

![The “Ready to go” list at the top of the front desk shows you the next steps.](/img/hilfe/tresen-startklar.jpg)

1. On a computer or tablet, open **“Today”**. This is the front desk, your view in the shop. If Dranzeit asks what you're using the device for, tap **“Front desk tablet”** or, for now, **“Later”**.
2. Scan the QR code in the “Ready to go” list with your phone and take a number, just as your customers will later. If you're signed in to Dranzeit on your phone, sign out there first.
3. The number appears immediately at the front desk under **“Up next”**.
4. At the front desk, tap **“Call next number”**. Your phone shows: **“It's your turn”**.
5. Tap **“Done”**. That's it.

How to get the test number out of your statistics again is explained under [Test run before your first customer](/hilfe/testlauf).

## 4. Put up the poster

Under **“Shop” → “Print poster”** you'll get a ready-made A4 poster with your QR code. Put it up where customers see it before they sit down: on the door, in the shop window or on the counter. More on this: [Print and put up the poster](/hilfe/plakat).

## 5. Your first real customer

Customers scan the code, choose a service and take a number. On their phone, they see how many people are ahead of them and roughly when it's their turn, and they can head out in the meantime. Shortly before it's their turn, they get notified if they've turned that on.

![How a customer sees their number: how many people are ahead of them and roughly when it's their turn.](/img/hilfe/kunde-nummer.jpg)

Is someone standing in front of you without a phone? Under “Today”, tap **“Add customer”**. Then you add them to the queue yourself. That way, everyone is in the same queue. More on this: [Issue a number at the front desk](/hilfe/nummer-vergeben).
