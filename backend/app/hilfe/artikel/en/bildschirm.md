The waiting-room display shows, for each person in the team, which number is being served right now and which are next. It updates itself. It's part of the Shop and Team plans and is included in the trial. Without a suitable plan, it only shows a notice.

## What's on it

- at the top, your shop's logo and name, with the time on the right
- for each person, **“Now serving”** with the number and **“Up next”** with up to four numbers and the approximate time
- on the right, a QR code for taking a number
- at the bottom, how many people are waiting right now

![The waiting-room display: who's being served, who's next, and the QR code for joining the queue.](/img/hilfe/bildschirm.jpg)

Customer names aren't shown there, only numbers. Appointments without a number appear as a dot. If you have more than four people in your team, it only shows those who are serving someone right now or have someone waiting.

## Opening it

1. Tap [Shop](/shop-profile) at the top and, next to the QR code, **“Open waiting-room display”**. If you have several shops, choose the right one at the top first.
2. The display opens in a new tab. Its address ends in /bildschirm/ followed by a long ID.
3. Open exactly this address on the device in the waiting area. You don't need to sign in there.

> **Tip:** If you can sign in once on the device in the waiting area, set it up: under **“Shop”** → **“On this device”** → **“Set up”** → **“Waiting room display screen”**. After that, it always opens the display when Dranzeit starts, even without signing in.

> **Important:** Anyone who knows the address can open the display. All it shows is numbers, times and the names of your team.

## Which device?

- **Smart TV:** Open the TV's browser, type in the address carefully once and save it as a bookmark.
- **Laptop via HDMI:** Connect the laptop to the TV, open the page and switch to full screen: **F11** on Windows, **Ctrl + Cmd + F** on a Mac.
- **Tablet:** Send yourself the address in a message, open it and turn the tablet sideways. The display is designed for landscape.

## Keeping it switched on

The page asks the browser not to turn off the screen. Not every device goes along with this, and many TVs don't. So switch off power saving mode, automatic switch-off and the screen lock on the device itself, and keep it plugged in.
