“My day” is the view for each person on the team. It shows only their own numbers and appointments, hour by hour. The front desk shows the whole shop, “My day” only you.

As an employee, you'll find “My day” in the menu at the top. If you're on the team at several shops, choose the shop at the top.

As the owner, you open it under **“Shop”** → **“My day”**: for your week, your working hours and **“Add customer”**. For today, it's easier to use **“Today”** with **“Only me”**, where you also call your customers. If you serve customers yourself, add yourself under **“Shop”** → **“Team”** with your email. Otherwise “My day” shows “Your team's appointments are at the front desk.”, and “Only me” is missing. How to bring others on board: [Invite your team](/hilfe/team-einladen).

## Choose the time range

Use the arrows at the top to go forwards and back; **“Today”** jumps back. Next to them, you choose **1 day**, **3 days**, **5 days** or **7 days**. Each day gets its own column; on a phone, you swipe sideways.

On the timeline, the red line is the current time, and your weekly breaks are hatched. Customers who are waiting have an outline, the one being served is yellow, and those who are done are dark.

## Add an appointment

1. Tap a free spot on the timeline. The **“Add appointment”** window opens with that time. Or tap **“+ Appointment”** at the top of the day, and the next quarter of an hour is preselected.
2. Enter the name or choose someone under **“Existing customer”**.
3. Choose the **Service** and check the **Duration**.
4. Tap **“Add appointment”**.

If the time doesn't work, for example because of a break, Dranzeit tells you why and doesn't save anything.

## Add customer

Tap **“Add customer”** at the top, enter the name and email and tap **“Add customer”**. The person then appears under **“Existing customer”** when you add an appointment.

Dranzeit then shows a link. Send it to the person with **“Copy link”** or **“Send by email”**, or on a phone with **“Share”**. If they open it and sign in or create an account, the appointments you add for them appear under “My appointments” in their account. There, they can switch on **“Earlier slot”** themselves for each upcoming appointment and will then be offered an earlier slot if one becomes free ([Earlier slot](/hilfe/frueher-dran)). The link is valid for 30 days, and Dranzeit only shows it this one time. If you need it again, for example because it has expired, add the person again with the same email: you'll get a new link, but not a second entry. The old link then no longer works.

Anyone who takes a number or books an appointment with you using an account automatically appears under **“Existing customer”** afterwards, already linked to their account. If you added the person beforehand with the same email, Dranzeit links your entry to their account once their address is confirmed there (via the link in our confirmation email). Otherwise, send them the link.

## A customer's appointments and notes

All your shop's customers are listed under **“Customers”** in the menu at the top, with all their appointments, revenue and notes such as the colour formula. That's also where you delete a customer. More: [Customers: your customer file](/hilfe/kunden)

## Moving appointments

Hold an appointment and drag it to the new spot: on a computer with the mouse, on a phone or tablet after pressing and holding briefly. A dashed outline shows the new time. Let go when it's right. If 3 or more days are visible, you can also drag it to another day.

If the new time is taken, the appointment stays where it was, for example with “Not moved: There's already an appointment then (10:00–10:30).” If the customer has a number page or an account, they'll see the new time there and get the message “New time” by push or email. They're also notified if you cancel an appointment. More on this: [Which messages your customers receive](/hilfe/benachrichtigungen).

## Status and conflicts

Tap an appointment to change its status: **Waiting**, **Being served**, **Done** or **Cancelled**. Cancelled ones disappear from the timeline.

If a red **“Conflict”** appears in front of an appointment, it falls within one of your weekly breaks, usually because the break was added later. Drag it to a free spot.

## My working hours

Under **“My working hours”**, you set for each day of the week whether you work and from when to when, then tap **“Save changes”**. Customers can only take a number or book an appointment with you during these times. The owner adds breaks under **“Shop”** → **“Team”**: [Working hours and breaks](/hilfe/arbeitszeiten-und-pausen).
