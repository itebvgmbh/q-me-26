Under [Shop](/shop-profile), in the **“On your website”** section, you'll find three ready-made building blocks. Pick one, tap **“Copy code”** and paste it into your website. Taking a number and booking always happen on Dranzeit itself, in a new tab. That way sign-in, the page with the number and the “It's your turn” message work as usual.

## Box

The box shows your shop with its name, whether it's open right now and how many people are waiting. Below it are the buttons **“Take a number”** and, if your plan includes appointments, **“Book an appointment”**. On wide screens, the QR code appears next to it so visitors on a computer can scan it with their phone.

The box updates itself. If you change your opening hours or name, you'll see that on your website too.

The box loads from dranzeit.de. As with any page visit, our server sees the visitors' IP address; the box doesn't set any cookies. So mention it in your privacy policy. You can copy a suggested sentence right below the code.

## Button

A simple link button in Dranzeit yellow. You choose where it leads: to taking a number, to booking or to your page on Dranzeit, where customers find both. You can change the label.

Until someone clicks the button, Dranzeit learns nothing. You don't need anything in your privacy policy for it. If your website builder can't handle HTML, create your own button there and enter the link shown below the code.

## QR code as an image

The same QR code as on the [poster](/hilfe/plakat): whoever scans it takes a number. Download it as a **PNG** for your website and Word, or as an **SVG** for the print shop. An SVG can be printed at any size without going blurry.

## Where the code goes

Paste the code where your website builder offers **“HTML”**, **“Code”** or **“Embed”**. In WordPress, that's the **“Custom HTML”** block. The box works well on your home page or next to your opening hours.

> **Tip:** Also add the link to your Dranzeit page to your Google Business Profile, as the link for appointments or bookings. You copy the link at the bottom of the “On your website” section. More on this: [Help customers find you](/hilfe/gefunden-werden).
