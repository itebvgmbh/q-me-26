Short answers to what shops ask most often before getting started. If your question isn't here, write to us at [hilfe@dranzeit.de](mailto:hilfe@dranzeit.de).

### What does Dranzeit cost, and do you take commission?

There's no commission and there are no fees per customer or appointment. You pay a fixed price per shop: Free costs nothing (one person, up to 100 customers a month), Solo €19, Shop €39 and Team €59 a month, all excl. VAT. If you pay yearly, you pay 10 times the monthly price. All the details: [Which plan suits me?](/hilfe/tarife) What Dranzeit brings you each month on balance: [Savings calculator](/rechner)

### Do my customers need an app or an account?

No. They scan the QR code on your door, take a number in their browser and only give their first name. They only need an account for “Earlier slot”. They can also book a fixed appointment without an account if your plan includes online appointments. More: [How the number works](/hilfe/so-funktioniert-die-nummer)

### What about customers without a smartphone?

You add them to the queue yourself under **“Today”** with **“Add customer”**. They're then in the same queue as everyone else, and you immediately see roughly what time it'll be their turn. More: [Issue a number at the front desk](/hilfe/nummer-vergeben)

### How long can I try it out, and what happens afterwards?

30 days with all features, without payment details. The trial ends on its own and costs nothing. After that, your shop carries on in the Free plan until you choose a subscription; nothing is deleted. More: [Trial](/hilfe/testphase)

### Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Under **“Shop”** → **“Plan”** → **“Manage subscription, payment method, cancel”**, you cancel at the end of the paid month or year. After that, your shop carries on in the Free plan. More: [Take out, change and cancel a subscription](/hilfe/abo)

### What devices do I need?

For the front desk, a phone, tablet or computer with internet and a browser; you don't need to install anything. Which tablet is worth it: [Which tablet for the front desk?](/hilfe/tablet-fuer-den-tresen). Plus, print the QR poster on A4 once. For the [waiting-room display](/hilfe/bildschirm), a TV or screen with a browser is enough.

The first time you open **“Today”**, Dranzeit asks what you use the device for: **“Front desk tablet”**, **“My phone”**, **“Waiting room display screen”** or **“Office”**. After that, it always starts there. You can change this under **“Shop”** → **“On this device”**.

### Does it work with appointments and walk-ins at the same time?

Yes. Appointments and numbers end up in the same day, per person. A number gets the next free slot between the appointments, so nothing gets in the way. Your customers can book online appointments from the Solo plan upwards. More: [My day](/hilfe/mein-tag)

### What happens if the internet goes down?

Dranzeit runs in the browser and needs internet; there's no offline mode. If your Wi-Fi goes down, no new numbers arrive at the front desk until the connection is back. Everything is saved online: numbers taken and appointments aren't lost, and customers with mobile data can keep taking numbers. The quickest fix is to switch the front desk device to mobile data.

### How many people and shops can I add?

Free and Solo are for one person, Shop for up to 4 and Team for up to 10 people, including you if you serve customers. You run several shops with one account; each shop has its own plan. More than 10 people? Write to us. More: [Several shops](/hilfe/mehrere-laeden)

### What about data protection?

Your customers only give their first name; mobile number and email address are optional. For numbers and appointments without an account, we remove names and contact details 90 days after the appointment. We delete customers in your customer list who have no linked account once there hasn't been an appointment with them for 24 months, or you delete them beforehand under **“Customers”**. The data processing agreement is in section 7 of the [terms](/agb). More: [Privacy: what data Dranzeit stores](/hilfe/datenschutz)

### How do new customers find me?

Your shop appears on the map under **“Find shops”**, with services, prices and opening hours. Hairdressers and barbershops with an address also appear on the “Walk-in hairdressers in …” pages for their town. On site, the QR poster on the door brings in walk-in customers. More: [Get found](/hilfe/gefunden-werden)
